Work Process

The pentesting methodology I'm training myself to follow, in order.

  1. 01

    Reconnaissance

    Gather information about the target — passively and actively — before touching anything.

  2. 02

    Scanning

    Use tools like Nmap to identify live hosts, open ports, and running services.

  3. 03

    Enumeration

    Dig deeper into discovered services to understand versions, configs, and possible weak points.

  4. 04

    Vulnerability Analysis

    Compare findings against known vulnerabilities to identify real, exploitable risk.

  5. 05

    Reporting

    Document findings clearly — what was found, how, and what should be done about it.
